Privacy policy

S1 backup uses the Google Drive scope drive.file. It can only see and change files it created itself in the Google Drive of the account that authorised it. It cannot read any other files.

Backups are encrypted on the owner's own computer before upload. No data is collected, shared, sold or sent anywhere other than that Google Drive.

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account under Security, Third-party connections.
